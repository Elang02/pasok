The class of each SKU

Every SKU carries a class built from two parts. The letter says how much the SKU adds to sales, so A adds the most and C the least. Low or Med says how steady those sales are from month to month, so Low barely moves and Med moves more.

Stock that expires before it sells

A SKU is listed here when the stock on hand would still be sitting on the shelf after its own expiry date. The line is the shelf life the catalogue gives that SKU.

Stock that sells slowly

A SKU is listed here when the stock on hand covers more than ninety days of sales at the rate it sells.
