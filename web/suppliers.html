A supplier who takes longer than the rest and delivers less than the order is the most common reason stock runs out. This page names them, shows what each supplier covers, what they have promised to send and when, and what we have already sent them.

How each supplier delivers

Read from the delivery records. The supplier who delivers the smallest part of the order is first. A marked line delivers less than the order.

What each supplier covers

Every item is bought from exactly one supplier, and one supplier carries many items at once, so the delivery time for an item can be read from the supplier that carries it.

What the suppliers have promised to send

Each date below is a day a supplier has promised a delivery. This stock is already on its way to the depots.

What we have sent them

Every notice we have sent a supplier, and the ones still waiting for a person to approve them.
