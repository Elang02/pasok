Stock that has already been bought is the cheapest stock in the network. It only costs money where it sits and does not sell. A case that waits for demand in the central depot is a case a city depot sells in days. Moving it costs a truck. Buying the same goods again costs the full price and then waits for a delivery. So the loop moves the stock it already owns first, and places an order only when there is nothing left worth moving.
