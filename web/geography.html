The cities the network sells into

Read from the city demand and the outlet list behind the answers. Every figure on this page is fetched from the network, so the page cannot print a number the files do not hold.

Where the goods sell

One row per city, the biggest demand first. The bar is that city's share of everything the network asked for. The five goods below each city are the ones it asks for most, and the letter after each SKU is its class, where A adds the most to sales.

Stock that sits above the target

A SKU holds extra stock when it covers more days than the plan asked for, and that stock sits in the central depot. The loop moves the extra stock to another city instead of buying it again, and it picks the city where the goods sell fastest. The value below is the extra stock each city depot is in line to take.

Every move the loop has made is listed on the Transfers page.
