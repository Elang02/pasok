Every day the loop looks at what sold, what is left in stock, and how each supplier has been delivering. From that it picks the SKUs that need more, places the order, and tells the principal. When the goods arrive, it compares what came with what it expected. The next day starts from there.

What it checks

  • A stock running outIt orders when the stock would run out before the next delivery arrives. Of 121 orders, 91.7% went out without a person touching them.
  • Demand jumpingIt orders more when demand is about to run above normal. The forecast misses about 12.8% of units sold, so no order is sized straight off it.
  • A supplier running lateIt orders earlier when a supplier has been delivering late. Suppliers deliver 91.2% of what we order, and the weakest are ordered from earlier.
  • A large orderIt waits for a person when the order value is above the limit. This run placed IDR 8.10M of orders, and the order rule keeps them swinging 72.9% less than ordering straight off the forecast.
